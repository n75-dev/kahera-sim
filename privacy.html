Privacy Policy for Kahera Simulator

Last updated: October 7, 2025

Kahera Simulator is developed by n75 and is provided as a free app with optional ads. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

The game itself does not collect personally identifiable information. However, Kahera Simulator uses Unity Ads, which may collect certain information, including but not limited to:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Usage data (e.g., interaction with ads, session information)

This information is collected to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to improve the functionality of the game.

How We Use Information

  • Displaying ads and supporting the development of the game
  • Improving game performance and user experience
  • Providing analytics to optimize ad delivery

Third-Party Services

Kahera Simulator uses Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). For more information about Unity Ads’ data collection and usage practices, please review their Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

You can control personalized ads and data collection through your device settings:

  • On Android: Google Play settings → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization

Please note that opting out of personalized ads does not remove ads entirely; you may still see non-personalized ads.

Children’s Privacy

Kahera Simulator is not intended for children under 13. Unity Ads provides tools and settings to comply with child-privacy laws, including COPPA.

Data Security

All data collected by Unity Ads is transmitted securely using encryption protocols. We do not sell or share personally identifiable information collected through the game.

Data Retention

Unity Ads may retain collected information as described in their Privacy Policy. We do not retain personal information outside of what is necessary to deliver ads and improve the game.
